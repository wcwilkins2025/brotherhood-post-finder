How to use it

  1. 1
    Be signed in to Skool. Open skool.com in your browser and sign in the way you normally do. You only need to do this once.
  2. 2
    Come back here and click any button below. It opens the Brotherhood in a new tab, already showing the posts on that topic.
  3. 3
    Scroll down past the long pinned posts. The member questions and stories are underneath. Click any post to read it or reply.

If a button shows a page with a Log In button instead of posts, you are not signed in yet. Sign in on that page, then click the button again.
